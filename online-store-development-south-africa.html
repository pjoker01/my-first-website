ONLINE STORE QUESTIONS

Can I sell products online in South Africa?

Yes. The store can be planned around your products, customers and fulfilment process. The exact ordering and payment setup depends on the providers and accounts available to your business.

Do I need to upload every product myself?

We can discuss the best way to prepare and add your catalogue. The work depends on how many products you have, the quality of the product information and whether it is already stored in a usable format.

Can customers order through WhatsApp?

An enquiry or WhatsApp ordering journey can be included where it suits your business. This is different from a full automated checkout, so we will make the customer journey clear before work begins.

Can you connect online payments?

Payment integration can be scoped when your business has an appropriate provider and account. Provider approval, transaction fees and any external service costs are separate from the website build.

Can the store show stock levels or delivery options?

These features depend on how your stock and delivery information are managed. We first check what data or provider access is available, then agree what can be connected reliably.

How much does an online store cost?

Store costs depend on catalogue size, design, ordering flow and integrations. Hosting and third-party services are considered separately. Share your requirements for a project-specific quote rather than relying on a one-size-fits-all price.
