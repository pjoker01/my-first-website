GOOD TO KNOW

Do I need a logo and professional photos?

Not necessarily. If you already have them, great. If you don't, we can work with the assets you have and discuss what additional content may be needed.

Will I be able to update my website?

That depends on the type of website we build. Static sites can be updated through the project files, while systems with a CMS or other editing tools can be discussed when required.

What happens after launch?

You'll have the agreed launch information and support terms. Ongoing changes, maintenance or additional features can be quoted separately.
