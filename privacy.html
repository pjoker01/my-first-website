08 / PRIVACY

Your information, your choice.

This Privacy Notice explains how PJ Web Development may handle information when you visit this website or choose to contact PJ. It reflects the website's current features and may be updated when those features or service providers change.

01 / WEBSITE USE

What this website collects.

The guided website quote helper prepares a message in your browser and opens WhatsApp; it does not send that message until you choose to send it in WhatsApp. The website also embeds the separate PJ Assistant chat at assistant.pjwebdev.online. When you type into that chat, your message is sent to the PJ Chat Platform to generate a reply and maintain the conversation.

The embedded assistant records limited service-usage events, such as widget opens, conversations started, lead captures, quote or booking requests and contact clicks. A temporary visitor identifier is stored in your browser sessionStorage to group these events for the session. The current site source does not include third-party advertising pixels or ad-retargeting scripts.

The hosting provider may process standard technical logs, such as requested pages, approximate request time, browser information and security events, to deliver and protect the website.

02 / WHEN YOU CONTACT PJ

Information you choose to share.

If you use the embedded PJ Assistant, the text you type is sent to the PJ Chat Platform and stored as conversation messages so the chat can respond and maintain its session history. If you choose to submit the assistant's enquiry form, the information may include your first name, surname, phone number, email address, enquiry summary, conversation reference and an optional screenshot.

If you send a WhatsApp message, PJ may receive your name, phone number, business name, website address, enquiry details and any files you decide to attach. This information is used to respond to enquiries, discuss requirements, prepare quotes, deliver agreed services and provide customer support.

03 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

WhatsApp and website hosting.

The PJ Assistant uses the PJ Chat Platform, which stores conversation and enquiry records in its database and may store optional screenshot attachments. The platform may send message content to configured AI providers, including Google Gemini and Groq, to generate assistant replies. Those providers process information under their own service terms and privacy policies.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta. When you follow a WhatsApp link, your interaction is subject to WhatsApp and Meta's own privacy terms. The website and assistant are hosted through Vercel, and the platform uses Supabase for database and file storage. These providers may process technical or service data as part of delivering and securing their services.

04 / RETENTION

How long information is kept.

Assistant conversation messages, usage events and voluntary enquiry records are stored in the PJ Chat Platform. A fixed public retention period is not currently stated. PJ aims to retain these records only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide agreed services, maintain necessary business records or meet legal obligations. You may contact PJ to ask about a particular record or request deletion where legally permitted.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity-document numbers or other sensitive personal information through the assistant or a general enquiry message.

YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

Ask, access or correct.

Choose what you share
You can browse this website without sending an enquiry. A WhatsApp message is sent only if you choose to send it from WhatsApp.

Request access or correction
If you have shared personal information with PJ, you may contact PJ to ask what information is held or request a correction.

Request deletion or object
You may ask PJ to delete information or raise an objection. Requests will be considered subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

South African privacy rights
PJ aims to handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

IMPORTANT NOTES

Does the embedded PJ Assistant store messages?

Yes. Messages you send to the embedded PJ Assistant are sent to the PJ Chat Platform and stored as conversation records. Optional enquiry-form submissions and screenshots may also be saved in the business enquiry queue.

Does opening WhatsApp send my message?

No. The guided quote helper opens WhatsApp with a prepared message, but PJ receives it only after you choose to send it in WhatsApp.

Does the assistant use analytics?

The embedded assistant records limited service-usage events such as widget opens, conversations, lead captures and quote or booking requests. It uses a temporary session identifier for these events. The current site source does not include third-party advertising pixels.
