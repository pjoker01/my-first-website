08 / PRIVACY

Your information, your choice.

This Privacy Notice explains how PJ Web Development may handle information when you visit this website or choose to contact PJ. It reflects the website's current features and may be updated when those features or service providers change.

01 / WEBSITE USE

What this website collects.

The current website does not submit enquiry details to a PJ server through a contact form. Its guided enquiry assistant prepares a message in your browser and opens WhatsApp only when you choose that option. PJ receives the message only if you decide to send it.

The hosting provider may process standard technical logs, such as requested pages, approximate request time, browser information and security events, to deliver and protect the website.

02 / WHEN YOU CONTACT PJ

Information you choose to share.

If you send a WhatsApp message, PJ may receive your name, phone number, business name, website address, enquiry details and any images or documents you decide to attach.

This information is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss requirements, prepare a quote, deliver agreed services and provide customer support.

03 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

WhatsApp and website hosting.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta. When you follow a WhatsApp link, your interaction is subject to WhatsApp and Meta's own privacy terms. PJ does not control how those providers process information on their platforms.

The website is hosted and delivered through Vercel. The hosting provider may process technical request and security information as part of operating the service.

04 / RETENTION

How long information is kept.

PJ aims to keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide agreed services, maintain necessary business records or meet legal obligations. The appropriate period depends on the enquiry and any resulting project.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive personal information through a general enquiry message.

YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

Ask, access or correct.

Choose what you share
You can browse this website without sending an enquiry. A WhatsApp message is sent only if you choose to send it from WhatsApp.

Request access or correction
If you have shared personal information with PJ, you may contact PJ to ask what information is held or request a correction.

Request deletion or object
You may ask PJ to delete information or raise an objection. Requests will be considered subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

South African privacy rights
PJ aims to handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

IMPORTANT NOTES

Does this website use analytics or advertising pixels?

No analytics or advertising-pixel scripts were found in the current public website source during the latest review. If analytics, advertising tools or additional tracking are introduced later, this notice should be updated.

Does PJ receive information when I open WhatsApp?

Opening WhatsApp does not itself send a message to PJ. PJ receives the information only after you choose to send the message in WhatsApp.

How do I make a privacy request?

Contact PJ Web Development on WhatsApp at +27 61 014 0065. Explain the request and include enough detail for PJ to identify the relevant enquiry or project.

PRIVACY QUESTIONS?

For a privacy question or request, contact PJ directly. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.
