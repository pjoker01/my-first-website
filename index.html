05 / QUICK ANSWERS

Before you start.

A few things clients usually want to know before choosing a package or starting a website project.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. Tell PJ what your business does and what you want the website to achieve. The technical side is handled for you.

Do I need to provide all the content?

Usually you'll provide your business information, photos, logo if you have one, services or products and contact details. We can work through anything you're missing.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Yes. The packages are starting points, so a simple website can grow with your business when you need more pages or features.

Are hosting and a domain included?

It depends on the package and project requirements. Suitable static websites may use free hosting; domains and paid third-party services are quoted separately when needed.
