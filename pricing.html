BEFORE YOU START

Are these prices final?

These are fixed package prices for the scope shown. If you need extra pages, custom features or other work outside the package, I'll give you a clear quote before starting.

Do I need to pay for hosting and a domain?

The domain is normally a separate annual cost and belongs to you. Suitable static sites may use free hosting; paid hosting is used when required. PJoker Care is optional ongoing website management at R299/month.

How many revisions do I get?

The revision allowance should be agreed in your quote before the project starts. Extra work outside the agreed scope can be quoted separately.

Can you build something outside these packages?

Yes. Send the idea. Custom websites and features can be quoted according to the actual requirements.
