WHAT AFFECTS THE PRICE?

Every project is different.

Final pricing can change depending on the number of pages, amount of content, design complexity and any custom functionality.

Hosting, domain registration, paid third-party services, copywriting, photography and advanced integrations can be separate costs when required.

BEFORE YOU START

Is the price on this page the final price?

No. These are starting prices. I'll confirm the scope and give you a project quote before we begin.

Do I need to pay for hosting and a domain?

A website needs somewhere to live and a domain may cost extra. Free hosting can be used for suitable projects; paid domains and services are separate unless included in your quote.

How many revisions do I get?

The revision allowance should be agreed in your quote before the project starts. Extra work outside the agreed scope can be quoted separately.

Can you build something outside these packages?

Yes. Send the idea. Custom websites and features can be quoted according to the actual requirements.
