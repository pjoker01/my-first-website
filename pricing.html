BEFORE YOU START

What does the R750 Starter package include?

The Starter package is a simple one-page business website with mobile design, WhatsApp, Google Maps, basic SEO and SSL/HTTPS. It is designed for businesses that need a professional online presence at an accessible starting price.

Do I need to pay for hosting and a domain?

The domain is normally a separate annual cost and belongs to you. Suitable static sites may use free hosting; paid hosting is used when required. PJ Care is optional ongoing website management at R299/month.

Can I upgrade later?

Absolutely. Start with the Starter package and upgrade when your business needs more pages, features or a larger website.

Are these prices final?

These are starting package prices for the scope shown. Extra pages, custom features, paid services or work outside the package will be quoted clearly before starting.

Can you build something outside these packages?

Yes. Send the idea. Custom websites and features can be quoted according to the actual requirements.
