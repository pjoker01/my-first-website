COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You explain your business and what you want the website to achieve. PJoker handles the technical side.

What do I need to provide?

Usually your business name, logo if you have one, photos, services or products, contact details and any social-media links. If something is missing, we can work through it.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can improve the visual design, mobile experience, structure and customer journey while keeping the useful information.

Can my website have WhatsApp, maps or ordering?

Yes. These can be included where they fit the project. More advanced features are quoted separately.
