COMMON QUESTIONS

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You explain your business and what you want the website to achieve. PJ handles the technical side.

What do I need to provide?

Usually your business name, logo if you have one, photos, services or products, contact details and any social-media links. If something is missing, we can work through it.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can improve the visual design, mobile experience, structure and customer journey while keeping the useful information.

Can you add an AI chatbot to my website?

Yes. A tailored assistant can answer common questions and guide visitors. The exact knowledge, integrations and usage limits are agreed before building.

Can you build an AI bot for an online store?

Yes. We can scope a shopping assistant that helps customers explore products and understand product details. Access to live catalogue data, stock, order status or checkout requires suitable integrations and is quoted separately.

Can you connect payments or booking systems?

Potentially, depending on the provider, account requirements and budget. We agree on the flow and any third-party fees before work starts.

Can my website have WhatsApp, maps or ordering?

Yes. These can be included where they fit the project. More advanced features are quoted separately.
