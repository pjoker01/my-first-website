COMMON SUPPORT QUESTIONS

What does PJ Care at R299/month include?

PJ Care includes website hosting/management, domain management assistance, SSL/HTTPS, minor text or image updates, basic monitoring and technical support. It includes up to two small content updates per month.

Are new pages or major changes included in PJ Care?

No. Major redesigns, new pages, new features and work outside the included minor updates are quoted separately before work begins.

Who owns my domain name?

Domains are registered in the customer's name. Domain renewal and paid third-party services are billed at their actual cost unless your written quote says otherwise.

How do I request help?

Use the WhatsApp contact link on this page. Include your website address, the issue and any helpful screenshots or details.

Can I ask for a new feature or a redesign through support?

Yes. PJ can discuss the request, but new features, redesigns and additional pages are treated as new scoped work and quoted separately.
