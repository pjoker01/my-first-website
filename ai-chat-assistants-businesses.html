AI ASSISTANT QUESTIONS

Does an AI assistant know everything about my business automatically?

No. It needs accurate business information and clear instructions. We agree what sources it can use, what it should not answer and where it should send customers when it is unsure.

Can I use it on my existing website?

That depends on the website platform and how it allows external tools or embedded components. We check compatibility before promising an installation method.

Can it answer questions about products?

It can answer from product information that is made available to it. Live stock, changing prices, order status and checkout require suitable integrations and should be tested separately.

Can it collect customer details?

Lead capture can be included when the required fields, destination and consent wording are agreed. We should only collect information that the business needs for the enquiry.

Will it replace my staff?

It is better treated as a first-line information assistant, not a replacement for people. Complex, sensitive or uncertain questions should have a clear route to a human.

Can it make mistakes?

Yes. AI systems can misunderstand questions or produce incorrect answers. Good setup includes approved information, clear limits, testing and a human contact option.

How much does a business AI assistant cost?

Pricing depends on the knowledge sources, expected usage, channels and integrations. Any third-party platform or usage charges are discussed separately before work starts.
